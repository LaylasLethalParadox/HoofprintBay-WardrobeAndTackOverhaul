Wardrobe and tack overhaul 1.0.1 for Horses of Hoofprint Bay.

Fixes the rider losing clothes. In 1.0.0 the Normal and Competition outfits could lose their jacket, breeches and boots at a random moment after a ride or after using the wardrobe tabs. You then rode competitions in your underwear and got "Inappropriate clothing" in the prestige results. 1.0.1 writes outfits and tack the same way the game does, so they stay as you set them.

If your rider already lost clothes in 1.0.0, open the wardrobe and dress them once more. Your saved outfits per activity, tack sets and halters are not affected.

Switching between Normal Outfit and Competition Outfit while the wardrobe is open also no longer leaves an activity's clothes on the other outfit.

Requires BepInEx 6 bleeding edge build 793 (IL2CPP, Windows x64). Mod Manager is optional. Made for game version 1.0.5. To update, extract the zip into the game folder and replace the old `WardrobeAndTackOverhaul.dll`.
