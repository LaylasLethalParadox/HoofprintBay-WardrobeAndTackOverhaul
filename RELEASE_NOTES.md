Wardrobe and tack overhaul 1.1.0 for Horses of Hoofprint Bay.

You can now buy halters in a colour of your own. Once the equipment store sells the game's custom colour ear bonnet, its ear bonnet list also has a "Custom Color Halter with Lead Rope" for 60. Pick the colour in the store's own colour picker, then pay at the counter as usual. You can buy as many colours as you want, each one once. Every colour you buy gets its own button on the Halter tab in Edit Tack, and with many colours the buttons go into several columns.

On walking trails such as the Forest Walk, the lead rope in your rider's hand now has the colour of the horse's halter. A horse you never picked a halter for keeps the game's rope.

Your saved outfits, tack sets and halters from 1.0.1 carry over.

Requires BepInEx 6 bleeding edge build 793 (IL2CPP, Windows x64). Mod Manager is optional. Made for game version 1.0.5. To update, extract the zip into the game folder and replace the old `WardrobeAndTackOverhaul.dll`.
